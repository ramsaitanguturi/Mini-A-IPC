## Priority 2: Fix statistical calculations and report generation

The generated report currently:
- Displays standard deviation as 0.00 despite nonzero CSV values.
- Confuses minimum latency with median latency.
- Reports incorrect adaptive regret.
- Does not clearly distinguish quick and full benchmark results.

Fix:
- Mean
- Median
- P90
- P95
- P99
- Minimum
- Maximum
- Standard deviation
- 95% confidence interval

Preserve correct units and precision.

Calculate adaptive performance regret using:

regret_percent = ((adaptive_latency - best_latency) / best_latency) * 100

A negative result means adaptive is faster than the best standalone transport measured.

Do not report 0% regret unless the measured difference is genuinely zero.

Also distinguish:
- Selection accuracy: whether the expected transport was selected.
- Performance regret: how much slower or faster the adaptive implementation was.

Do not confuse the two.
