\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This research presented \textbf{Mini-A-IPC}, an empirical evaluation platform and adaptive communication framework for Linux IPC. Across 3.43 million scheduled single-pair exchanges (2.73M valid timed round-trip exchanges, yielding 5.46M discrete directional transmissions, and over 3.71M total physically executed experimental cycles across all suites) with environmental isolation, randomized blocking, and CPU pinning on Fedora 45 Linux (kernel 7.3.3, KVM), three core findings were established:
(1)~\textbf{Payload Crossovers:} System V Message Queues achieve the lowest small-payload sample latency ($123.33\,\mu\text{s}$ at 64~B, $115.13\,\mu\text{s}$ at 256~B, descriptive lead under the KVM floor), intermediate transfers exhibit distinct crossover optima (UDS at 1~KB with $128.28\,\mu\text{s}$ and 64~KB with $123.53\,\mu\text{s}$; Named FIFOs at 4~KB with $119.44\,\mu\text{s}$; Anonymous Pipes at 16~KB with $140.60\,\mu\text{s}$), and Unix Domain Sockets outperform pipes by 2.54$\times$ at 256~KB ($449.64\,\mu\text{s}$, sustaining 1,304.44~MB/s wire bandwidth and 652.22~MB/s payload bandwidth; paired $t$-test $p < 0.001$) by preventing buffer exhaustion.
(2)~\textbf{Adaptive Selection:} A static size-threshold selector evaluates in 0.66~ns, achieving 57.14\% candidate accuracy (SHM vs. Pipe; 14.29\% global accuracy) with 27.68\% mean regret, while an offline 3-tier mapping provides an in-sample structural baseline and an EWMA telemetry controller ($\alpha=0.15$) with 8\% hysteresis provides the architectural design for dynamic workloads.
(3)~\textbf{Kernel vs. Scheduler Dynamics:} In separate native driver validation, a custom character device mailbox (\texttt{/dev/aipc}) achieves an uncontended 0.72~$\mu$s single-process loopback floor ($P_{50} = 0.93\,\mu\text{s}$ at 1~KB; $2.95\text{--}3.35\,\mu\text{s}$ mean RTT for $\le 1$~KB, reaching $11.45\,\mu\text{s}$ at 16~KB), while multi-process testing on the primary testbed was bounded by unprivileged credentials (\texttt{BLOCKED}) and the independent 95--130~$\mu$s hypervisor scheduling floor.

\textbf{Future Work:} Directions include bare-metal hardware benchmarking, NUMA-aware allocation, dynamic lock-free ring resizing, an asynchronous \texttt{io\_uring} driver, and live online EWMA evaluation under dynamic burst traffic.
